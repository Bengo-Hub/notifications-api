# Notifications sprints

Sprint files for notifications-api live in this folder.

## Find, Flip, Fix

Before planning or building a sprint here, research the products people already pay for in this area: how they sell, what they charge and what their customers complain about. Change the angle for our market and turn the top complaints into requirements a test can prove. Record the findings, with sources and dates, in the sprint file. Research that names a client stays in the private docs repo. Persuasive copy and flows need a true fact behind every claim.
